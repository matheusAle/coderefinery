§ the math · every verdict, by hand
Three numbers. Three rules.
No opinions.
Every verdict coderefinery gives is a formula you can check with a pencil. This page shows what each gate counts, how it scores, where its threshold comes from, and what the number quietly implies. All of it as implemented, byte-identical to crapper, dryer and mutator.

rule
